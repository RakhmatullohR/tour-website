DejaVu Fonts — bundled here deliberately (IMAGE-BRIEF-SPEC §5.4).

WHY BUNDLED: sharp renders SVG text through librsvg + fontconfig, which resolves
fonts from the HOST machine. On a bare CI container a system font may be absent
and the placeholders would render UNLABELLED with no error. Referencing this
bundled file explicitly makes placeholder generation hermetic.

COVERAGE VERIFIED: U+02BB (oʻ/gʻ modifier turned comma), U+02BC (ʼ), and Cyrillic.
